Jun 14, 1985 – I hope the Reds have enjoyed their time on Malden – because they don’t have much longer here. We’ve pushed them right back to the far North of the island, and it’s only a matter of time before we run them right into the sea.

We’re preparing for an offensive, but it’s difficult to use tanks in this rocky area, and choppers have been pretty useless thanks to the dense areas of woodland around here. We need more soldiers on the ground. So, while the garrison in Goisse waits for reinforcements, I’m going with a few men to the hills to find the enemy encampment. We can't attack if we don't know where the enemy is.

After our success here in Goisse, I’m just starting to feel that the tide is turning our way in this fight…

Lieutenant Armstrong, we need to find the camp from which the Soviets launched their attack on Goisse. Your men's job is to pinpoint its exact location and report back. Do not engage the enemy – the attack will follow later.

You’ll begin this mission at NATO's base in Goisse.

Follow the M113s, supporting their attack on the enemy position at grid Da30.

Find out the exact position of the enemy encampment, which we know is somewhere in the valley.

Get back to Goisse in one piece.
