Training details

To: Lieutenant Robert Hammer
From: Major W. Benjamin
We do appreciate your joining us at such short notice, Lieutenant. With things going the way they are, we need every good man we can get, regardless of experience.
Tank training consists of three elements:
1. Driving. You have to drive your tank to La Trinite.
2. Commanding. Your crew will join you in La Trinite. Proceed to the training grounds.
3. Attacking. There are some armored targets on the training grounds. You will be ordered to destroy them.

During this exercise you will be under the command of Major Sutherland. He will probably join you in your tank to see how are you doing.

Back to notes

The Soviets have got us on the run. To hit back we need tanks – and officers to command them. Officers like you, Lieutenant Hammer.

You’ll start at the NATO camp.

Get into the M60 as the driver and take it to La Trinite.

Quickly complete your training.

Travel to Dourdan, where your commander is attempting to hold the town.

Rendezvous with your commander and stop the enemy attack!
