June 7, 1985 - "Commander Gastovski," they say. "We're in trouble."

"Of course you are," I tell them. "If you weren't, I wouldn't be here."

Trouble and me - we go way back.

And this time it's the Russians, on some godforsaken little island. Sounds crazy, I know - but, hey, that's trouble for you. Always popping up where you least expect it.

Anyway, I've got a job to do here; clearing this pass for our tanks. They tried to get through yesterday, but Ivan beat 'em off, and we sustained heavy losses. So now it's up to me to get in there and clear the way. 

The Major's given me some written instructions, but the job seems pretty straightforward. In and out, causing as much mayhem as I can - just the way I like it.
